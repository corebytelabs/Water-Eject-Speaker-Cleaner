How to use it

Takes less than a minute.

Remove the case

Take off the phone cover so water can get out freely.

Volume to max

The app reminds you and helps set the volume.

Speaker facing down

Hold the phone so gravity helps the water drip out.

Start cleaning

Tap Start and repeat a few times if the sound is still muffled.

Built to be safe

We care about your speaker as much as you do.

1.5 s

Soft fade-in, so the speaker is never hit by sudden full power.

Time limits

Safe cycle lengths with rest breaks to avoid overheating.

100% local

Tones are generated on your phone. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Please note: the app uses sound vibrations to help remove water and loose dust. It can't repair hardware damage. For serious water damage, please visit a repair center.
