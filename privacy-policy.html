Water Eject & Speaker Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Water Eject & Speaker Cleaner, developed by CoreByteLabs ("we", "our", or "the app"), package name com.watereject.speakercleaner, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app operates 100% locally and offline on your device. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts).

2. Device Permissions and Usage

The app requests only minimal, necessary device permissions strictly required for its core functionality:

  • VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to generate synchronized haptic vibration pulses during cleaning cycles to help physically dislodge trapped water droplets and dust particles.
  • MODIFY AUDIO SETTINGS (android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used solely to route sound waves between your device's main loudspeaker and the earpiece (top call receiver) when using Earpiece Cleaner mode.

No audio is recorded, captured, or transmitted. The app only generates mathematical sound frequencies locally.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • The app operates completely offline.
  • If advertising or analytics SDKs are integrated in future updates, they will strictly adhere to Google Play Developer Policies, and this policy will be updated accordingly.
  • No personal data or sensitive device identifiers are sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Security & Safety

The app is engineered with built-in safety measures:

  • 1.5-second soft audio fade-in to protect speaker diaphragms from mechanical shock.
  • Safe cycle time limits to prevent thermal stress on internal speaker coils.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
